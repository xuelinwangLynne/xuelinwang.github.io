# Asset sources

- Xuelin Wang, Yuxin Hao, Qihao Yang and QQ: supplied by the site owner in the local photos folder.
- QQ’s current mascot: generated watercolor animation sheet using the owner’s photographs and supplied illustration as references. See QQ-ANIMATION.md for the exact prompt and frame layout. The original photo and earlier vector are retained as source assets.
- Chinese name: Yuji Boku brush typeface, Google Fonts, licensed under SIL OFL 1.1. The font is subset to 王雪琳; see fonts/YujiBoku-OFL.txt and https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/yujiboku.
- Pacifico: the same typeface used by https://charlesyang030.github.io/ . Font file from that site's public assets; font is licensed under SIL OFL 1.1 (see fonts/Pacifico-OFL.txt and https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/pacifico).
- Jinan University logo: https://www.jnu.edu.cn/2927/list.htm ; image https://www.jnu.edu.cn/_upload/tpl/00/f5/245/template245/images/list/logo.png .
- GDUFS logo: official university Logo and Motto page https://www.gdufs.edu.cn/AboutGDUFS/LogoandMotto.htm .
- UMass Amherst seal: official 2023 seal, described at https://www.umass.edu/news/article/new-university-seal-and-brand-mark-unveiled-umass-amherst-providing-distinctive ; downloaded via https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:UMass_Seal_Medium_PMS_202.png (original author: University of Massachusetts Amherst).

Institutional marks identify academic affiliation and education only.

## V4 vectors and icons

- Jinan and GDUFS emblems: vector paths from the official marks mirrored at https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Jinan_University_Emblem.svg and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Guangdong_University_of_Foreign_Studies_Emblem.svg .
- Huaqiao emblem: official source https://culture.hqu.edu.cn/yxhy/xh.htm ; traced to transparent SVG paths.
- Tongji emblem: official mark mirrored at https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Tongji_Uni_logo.svg ; embedded raster traced to transparent vector curves.
- UMass current seal: existing official 2023 mark traced to transparent vector paths.
- Google Scholar and ResearchGate icons: Academicons glyphs supplied by the existing academic homepage repository (SIL OFL 1.1). https://jpswalsh.github.io/academicons/ .
